Presentation notes
- Present at least:
- headline ASR
- constrained ASR or source-preserving ASR
- clean difficulty summary
- difficulty-stratified results
- Avoid presenting only one pooled ASR number
- Verification results are easiest to digest when tied back to the decision threshold
- Useful derived reporting targets include mean clean margin, residual margin, FAR-conditioned ASR, and clean-margin bins
