Core verification / recognition

  Link: https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_iccv_2015/html/Liu_Deep_Learning_Face_ICCV_2015_paper.html
  Used for: CelebA dataset reference.

Attack evaluation / further reading

- [active] Huang, Y. et al. (2025). AdvFaceGAN: a face dual-identity impersonation attack method based on generative adversarial networks. PeerJ Computer Science.
  Link: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12192821/
  Used for: motivating dual reporting of target success and source-identity preservation; useful for constrained ASR style thinking.
  Notes: Strongly relevant to the "attack should still look like the source" discussion.

- [active] Kessler, C. et al. (2024). Face morphing attack vulnerability / pair-selection analysis paper.
  Link: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11142600/
  Used for: supporting clean-pair difficulty stratification; useful evidence that embedding-based pair similarity changes attackability.
  Notes: Helpful when justifying clean-margin bins and not relying on a single pooled ASR.

- [active] Altuncu, D. et al. (2025). Advancing facial recognition security through adversarial attack research and standardization. Scientific Reports.
  Link: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-15753-8
  Used for: threshold-aware reporting language; useful when tying attack evaluation back to FAR/TAR style verification metrics.
  Notes: Good bridge reference between attack work and verification evaluation practice.

- [active] Wang, X., Wu, Y. C., Zhou, M., and Fu, H. F. (2024). Beyond surveillance: privacy, ethics, and regulations in face recognition technology. Frontiers in Big Data.
  Link: https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/big-data/articles/10.3389/fdata.2024.1337465/full
  Used for: broader context and framing; not central for metrics, but useful for motivation and discussion sections.
  Notes: Keep as context rather than as a technical metric source.
